\begin{abstract}
A companion Fourier--Galerkin Navier--Stokes observability programme shows that total- and
shell-energy Lie jets can generically determine a finite state modulo spatial translation in
several exactly certified finite cases, but it leaves quantitative inversion and noisy recovery
open.  This note isolates the role of nonlinear energy transfer in those open problems.  The
main correction is negative but useful: for prescribed forcing, shell transfer
$T=\dot I+2\nu S I-F$ is an invertible affine reparameterization of the first shell-energy jet,
so transfer variables do not create observability rank beyond $(I,\dot I)$.  Their advantage is
instead structural and numerical.  They make explicit the internal redistribution that total
energy cancels, and they admit a derivative-free finite-window balance with direct interval-error
propagation.  We also prove a constructive kinematic lemma: every Fourier mode added on the
boundary of a cubic cutoff $N\ge2$ belongs to a non-collinear Navier--Stokes triad containing a
mode from the previous cutoff.  This removes lack of triad connectivity as an obstruction to the
all-resolution saturation conjecture, while leaving algebraic independence of observable minors
open.  Finally, the programme's analytic three-mode subcase is upgraded to an explicit
quantitative inverse radius.  These are partial closures of the inner-certificate frontier, not a
proof of all-resolution observability, global injectivity, continuum regularity, or the Clay
Millennium problem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and claim discipline}

Let $K_N=\{k\in\mathbb Z^3:0<\|k\|_\infty\le N\}$ be a finite cubic Fourier cutoff for periodic
incompressible Navier--Stokes.  This note concerns the finite Fourier--Galerkin system and its
energy readouts.  Standard Navier--Stokes energy identities are used as standard mathematics and
are not claimed as original results.

The work is motivated by a Readout Genesis interpretation in which restoration and transmitted
difference are distinct roles.  That interpretation is not used as a premise for physical energy.
In particular, no informational quantity is identified with SI Joules without a separate domain
translation.  All mathematical claims below are established directly in the finite NS model.

\section{Shell balance: transfer versus dissipation}

For a squared-radius shell label $s$, let
\[
I_s(t)=\frac12\sum_{|k|^2=s}|\widehat u_k(t)|^2
\]
under the declared Fourier normalization.  The finite shell balance can be written
\begin{equation}\label{eq:balance}
\boxed{\dot I_s=T_s-2\nu s I_s+F_s,}
\end{equation}
where $T_s$ is the quadratic nonlinear transfer and $F_s$ is a prescribed shell-energy input.  In
the unforced closed finite truncation the nonlinearity conserves total kinetic energy, hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transferzero}
\boxed{\sum_sT_s=0.}
\end{equation}
Thus the nonlinear term redistributes energy among retained shells while the viscous term removes
it.  Summing~\eqref{eq:balance} over $s$ erases the internal transfer pattern by
\eqref{eq:transferzero}; retaining the individual shell equations keeps that pattern visible.

\section{Transfer is not an extra-rank oracle}

Write $I=(I_1,\ldots,I_m)^T$ and $S=\mathrm{diag}(s_1,\ldots,s_m)$.  For known viscosity and
prescribed forcing,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:transfer}
T=\dot I+2\nu S I-F.
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[First-jet/transfer equivalence]\label{prop:equiv}
For prescribed $F$, the observation pairs $(I,T)$ and $(I,\dot I)$ have the same local Jacobian
rank at every state where the derivatives are defined.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:transfer} gives
\[
\begin{bmatrix}I\\T\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}I_m&0\\2\nu S&I_m\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}I\\\dot I\end{bmatrix}
-
\begin{bmatrix}0\\F\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The block triangular matrix has determinant one and is therefore invertible.  Multiplication by an
invertible output-coordinate map preserves Jacobian rank.
\end{proof}

For the exact $N=1$ modular witness already used by the observability programme, the companion
checker verifies
\[
\operatorname{rank}D(I,\dot I)=\operatorname{rank}D(I,T)=5,
\]
and verifies the differential transfer dependence $\sum_sDT_s=0$.  Hence transfer makes the
mechanism visible but does not bypass the shell-jet rank ceiling.

\section{Derivative-free finite-window transfer}

Integrating~\eqref{eq:balance} over $[t_0,t_1]$ yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window}
\boxed{
\int_{t_0}^{t_1}T_s\,dt
=I_s(t_1)-I_s(t_0)
+2\nu s\int_{t_0}^{t_1}I_s\,dt
-\int_{t_0}^{t_1}F_s\,dt.
}
\end{equation}
This avoids numerical differentiation of measured energy data.  If certified absolute interval
radii for the four right-hand terms are respectively $r_1,r_0,r_I,r_F$, interval arithmetic gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:windowrad}
\boxed{
\operatorname{rad}\!\left(\int_{t_0}^{t_1}T_sdt\right)
\le r_1+r_0+2\nu s r_I+r_F.
}
\end{equation}
This closes a specific noise-propagation subproblem: transfer-window uncertainty can be certified
without constructing high-order time derivatives.  It does not yet provide a certified inverse
from those windows to the complete Fourier state.

\section{All-cutoff kinematic triad connectivity}

\begin{lemma}[Boundary-to-old-cutoff triad connection]\label{lem:connect}
For every integer $N\ge2$ and every $k\in K_N$ with $\|k\|_\infty=N$, there exist
$p\in K_{N-1}$ and $q\in K_N$ such that
\[
p+q=k,\qquad p\times q\ne0.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Choose an index $i$ with $|k_i|=N$.  If $k$ has a nonzero component transverse to $i$, set
$p=\operatorname{sgn}(k_i)e_i$ and $q=k-p$.  Then $\|p\|_\infty=1\le N-1$, $q\in K_N$, and the
transverse component makes $p$ and $q$ non-collinear.  If instead $k=\pm Ne_i$, choose any
$j\ne i$, set $p=e_j$ and $q=k-p$.  Again $p\in K_{N-1}$, $q\in K_N$, and $p\times q\ne0$.
\end{proof}

For non-collinear $p,q$, suitable divergence-free polarizations give a nontrivial quadratic
Navier--Stokes interaction.  Thus every new boundary mode is kinematically attached to the previous
cutoff.  The repository checker exhaustively verifies the constructive choice through $N=8$.

\begin{remark}[What the lemma does not prove]
Lemma~\ref{lem:connect} does not prove nonzero transfer at every state, because modal amplitudes may
vanish and different triads may cancel.  It also does not prove independence of the observation
rows.  Therefore it narrows but does not solve the all-$N$ earliest-order saturation conjecture.
The remaining obstruction is algebraic: enough symmetry-reduced observation minors must be shown
generically nonzero.
\end{remark}

\section{A quantitative inverse in the analytic triad subcase}

The programme's existing three-mode analytic subcase uses reduced invariants $(x,y,z,r)$ and the
coefficient
\[
c_a=\frac{3}{10},\qquad |a|^2=1.
\]
Its first shell-energy derivative obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:triadjet}
J_{1,a}=2c_ar-2\nu x.
\end{equation}
Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rinv}
\boxed{r=\frac{J_{1,a}+2\nu x}{2c_a}.}
\end{equation}
If $x$ and $J_{1,a}$ have certified absolute radii $\sigma_x$ and $\sigma_J$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rrad}
\boxed{
|r-\widehat r|
\le\frac{\sigma_J+2\nu\sigma_x}{2|c_a|}.
}
\end{equation}
At $\nu=1/200$ this simplifies to
\[
|r-\widehat r|\le\frac53\sigma_J+\frac1{60}\sigma_x.
\]
The exact rational checker uses a perturbed sample and verifies an error of $49/60000$ against the
certified bound $17/20000$.

This is a genuine quantitative inner-inverse witness, but only for the reduced triad model.  It is
not a certified inversion of the 52-real-dimensional $N=1$ cube.

\section{Relation to the observable-to-continuum certificate}

The companion EPSC synthesis gives the conditional composition
\[
\inf_{g\in G}\|u(T)-g\widehat x_N\|_2
\le\sqrt{\rho_N^2+\beta_N^2},
\]
where $\rho_N$ is a certified retained-state reconstruction radius and $\beta_N$ is an independently
certified omitted-tail radius.  The present note advances the inner side in two ways: it supplies a
quantitative $\rho$-type bound in the exact reduced triad case, and it supplies derivative-free
window transfer data with explicit uncertainty radii.  Neither result manufactures the full
$\rho_N$ needed for a general finite Fourier state.

\section{Updated open problems}

The work leaves four deliberately separated frontiers.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Full finite inverse (EPSC-18).}  Fix the spatial-translation gauge, control observation
branches, and certify a quantitative inverse radius for the complete finite Fourier quotient.  The
triad result~\eqref{eq:rrad} is only a low-dimensional witness.
\item \textbf{Full noisy chain (EPSC-19).}  Propagate certified measurement-window errors through the
full finite inverse and then through the EPSC tail composition.  Equation~\eqref{eq:windowrad}
removes one differentiation bottleneck but not the inversion bottleneck.
\item \textbf{All-$N$ saturation (NSOBS-07).}  Kinematic connectivity is supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:connect}; the remaining issue is generic nonvanishing/algebraic independence of enough
symmetry-reduced observation minors.
\item \textbf{Outer-certificate scaling (EPSC-16).}  Improve the independent finite-to-continuum
certificate's cost and tightness at high cutoff and long horizon.  This is not the same problem as
observability inversion.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

Energy transfer is useful in finite Navier--Stokes observability, but not because it secretly adds
rank.  Its mathematically honest role is more specific: it is the coordinate in which internal
redistribution becomes explicit, the conservation dependence behind the shell-reader rank count is
visible, and finite-window observations can be certified without high-order differentiation.  The
new all-cutoff connectivity lemma removes a structural obstruction to the all-resolution
conjecture, while the reduced triad inverse gives a first explicit quantitative inner certificate.
The full finite-state inverse, its noisy extension, and all-$N$ minor independence remain open and
are stated as such.
