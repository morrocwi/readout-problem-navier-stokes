\begin{center}
{\bfseries\large ENERGY OBSERVABILITY ACROSS FOURIER--GALERKIN\\[2pt]
RESOLUTIONS OF THREE-DIMENSIONAL NAVIER--STOKES}

\vspace{0.27in}
{\small YAOHAREE LAHTEE}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.18in}

Consider the unforced periodic three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equation after
finite cubic Fourier--Galerkin truncation.  This note separates two questions that are often
conflated: how many simultaneous energy measurements are retained, and how deeply their time
evolution must be observed before they become locally complete modulo symmetry.  For every
finite cubic resolution we derive sharp structural ceilings for one scalar total-energy reader
and for the full shell-energy reader, and show that the resulting jet ranks are independent of
every positive viscosity.  Exact modular certificates attain the ceilings at the earliest
structurally possible orders in four tested cases: scalar energy at $N=1,2$ and shell energy at
$N=1,3$.  The finite results motivate a resolution-indexed saturation conjecture and an
explicit measurement--time trade-off.  No continuum regularity claim is made.

\section*{1. Resolution, readers, and all-$N$ ceilings}

For $N\ge1$, retain
\begin{equation}
K_N=\{-N,\ldots,N\}^3\setminus\{0\},
\end{equation}
with real phase-space dimension
\begin{equation}
\boxed{d_N=2\bigl((2N+1)^3-1\bigr).}
\end{equation}
Write
\begin{equation}
\dot x=F_\nu(x)=\nu Ax+B(x,x),\qquad \nu>0,
\end{equation}
where $B$ is the projected quadratic Navier--Stokes interaction.  Let
\begin{equation}
Q_N=\{|k|^2:k\in K_N\},\qquad m_N=|Q_N|,
\end{equation}
and let $I=(I_q)_{q\in Q_N}\in\R^{m_N}$ denote the exact shell energies.  Their sum is total
retained kinetic energy
\begin{equation}
E=\mathbf1^T I.
\end{equation}
Define the scalar and shell Lie jets
\begin{equation}
\mathcal E_R=(E,\Lie_FE,\ldots,\Lie_F^R E),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\mathcal J_R=(I,\Lie_FI,\ldots,\Lie_F^R I).
\end{equation}
Translation by $a\in\T^3$ acts as $(T_a\uHat)_k=e^{ik\cdot a}\uHat_k$.  The dynamics is
translation equivariant and both readers are invariant.  At generic states the translation
orbit is three-dimensional, so both jet ranks are at most $d_N-3$.

The scalar jet has $R+1$ coordinates.  For the shell reader, the exact shell balance is
\begin{equation}
J_1+L_\nu J_0=\eta^{NS},\qquad \mathbf1^T\eta^{NS}=0,
\end{equation}
where $L_\nu$ is the fixed shellwise viscous operator.  Repeated differentiation yields one
scalar dependence in each new $m_N$-component shell block.  Hence:

\begin{theorem}[Universal finite-resolution ceilings]
For every $N\ge1$, every $\nu>0$, and every $R\ge0$,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}D\mathcal E_R\le\min(R+1,d_N-3),}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}D\mathcal J_R\le
\min\bigl(m_N+(m_N-1)R,d_N-3\bigr).}
\end{equation}
Therefore the symmetry ceiling cannot be reached before
\begin{equation}
\boxed{R_E^{\min}=d_N-4,}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{R_I^{\min}=\ceil{\frac{d_N-3-m_N}{m_N-1}}.}
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}

\section*{2. Positive-viscosity universality}

The viscosity parameter does not generate a new rank problem at each positive value.

\begin{theorem}[Positive-viscosity rank universality]
Fix $N$ and either energy reader $h\in\{E,I_q:q\in Q_N\}$.  For every $r\ge0$ and $\nu>0$,
\begin{equation}
\Lie_{F_\nu}^r h(\nu y)=\nu^{r+2}\Lie_{F_1}^r h(y),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
D_x\Lie_{F_\nu}^r h(\nu y)=\nu^{r+1}D_y\Lie_{F_1}^r h(y).
\end{equation}
Consequently every scalar- and shell-energy jet rank is independent of the value of
$\nu>0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Set $x(t)=\nu y(s)$ and $s=\nu t$.  Since $B$ is quadratic,
$F_\nu(\nu y)=\nu^2F_1(y)$.  Each energy reader is quadratic, giving the displayed identities.
For $\nu>0$ the induced state and row scalings are invertible, so rank is unchanged.
\end{proof}

The endpoint $\nu=0$ is different.  In particular total kinetic energy is conserved for the
finite Euler system, so the scalar energy jet collapses to $(E,0,0,\ldots)$.

\section*{3. Exact saturation certificates}

The certificates propagate formal Taylor coefficients of the Galerkin flow and tangent
system.  For polynomial $F$ and reader $h$,
\begin{equation}
h(\Phi_t x)=\sum_{r\ge0}\frac{t^r}{r!}\Lie_F^r h(x).
\end{equation}
Thus over a prime field $\F_p$ with $p>R$, Taylor-block and Lie-jet Jacobians have identical
rank through order $R$.  A nonzero maximal minor modulo a good prime implies that the
corresponding characteristic-zero rational minor polynomial is not identically zero; the
universal ceilings then give equality of the generic characteristic-zero rank.

The exact certificates currently give:
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{c|r|c|r|r|r}
N&d_N&\text{reader}&m& R_{\min}&\text{certified rank}\\ \hline
1&52&\text{total energy}&1&48&49\\
1&52&\text{shell energies}&3&23&49\\
2&248&\text{total energy}&1&244&245\\
3&684&\text{shell energies}&18&39&681
\end{array}
\end{equation}
Every row reaches the translation ceiling $d_N-3$ at the earliest order allowed by the
corresponding structural bound.

For $N=2$, an exact projected Jacobian over $\F_{251}$ satisfies
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}_{\F_{251}}D\mathcal E_{244}=245.}
\end{equation}
For $N=3$, the shell set is
\begin{equation}
Q_3=\{1,2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11,12,13,14,17,18,19,22,27\},
\end{equation}
so $m_3=18$, and an exact projected calculation over $\F_{683}$ gives
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\operatorname{rank}_{\F_{683}}D\mathcal J_{39}=681.}
\end{equation}
The recorded $N=3$ milestones are
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrr}
R&0&5&10&20&30&35&38&39\\ \hline
\operatorname{rank}&18&103&188&358&528&613&664&681,
\end{array}
\end{equation}
which follow $\min(18+17R,681)$ at every recorded order.

\begin{proposition}[Certified finite saturation]
At the four reader--resolution pairs in the table, and for every $\nu>0$, the corresponding
energy jet has generic local rank $d_N-3$ at its earliest structurally possible order.
\end{proposition}

At generic states the translation action is free; for example, nonzero coefficients at the
three axial modes force a fixing translation to satisfy
$e^{ia_1}=e^{ia_2}=e^{ia_3}=1$.  Therefore rank $d_N-3$ means local completeness modulo
continuous translation: the kernel of the saturated jet differential equals the tangent
space of the translation orbit.  This is local, not global, injectivity.

\section*{4. Resolution-indexed conjecture and measurement--time trade-off}

The exact cases suggest that the structural ceilings may be generically sharp at every finite
resolution.

\begin{conjecture}[Optimal energy-readout saturation]
For every finite cubic truncation $K_N$ and every $\nu>0$,
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\rankgen D\mathcal E_{d_N-4}=d_N-3,}
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\rankgen D\mathcal J_{R_I^{\min}}=d_N-3.}
\end{equation}
\end{conjecture}

Only the certified rows above are proved; the conjecture is not a statement about
$N\to\infty$ or continuum Navier--Stokes.  Nevertheless, the all-$N$ ceilings already expose
an exact design trade-off.  A scalar reader requires at least
\begin{equation}
R_E^{\min}=d_N-4=2(2N+1)^3-6=\Theta(N^3)
\end{equation}
time derivatives before symmetry-limited completeness is even possible.  By contrast, the
$m_N$-channel shell reader has lower bound (12).  At $N=3$ this changes the minimum admissible
temporal depth from
\begin{equation}
\boxed{1\ \text{channel}:\ R\ge680}
\end{equation}
to the exactly saturated
\begin{equation}
\boxed{18\ \text{channels}:\ R=39.}
\end{equation}
Thus the finite theory makes precise a measurement--time principle:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\text{more simultaneous dynamical readout}\ \longleftrightarrow\
\text{less temporal observability depth}.}
\end{equation}
This does not yet identify a physical sensor layout: shell energies are spectral observables,
and stable recovery from noisy high-order derivatives remains open.  It does identify the
quantity that a practical sensor-design program should minimize: temporal depth subject to
saturating the symmetry-limited observable state.

\section*{5. Scope and verification record}

The results are finite-dimensional observability statements.  They do not prove continuum
regularity or singularity formation, global reconstruction, stable noisy differentiation, or
computational speed-up.  Their precise content is the combination of universal all-$N$
structural ceilings, positive-viscosity rank universality, and exact ceiling saturation at the
certified reader--resolution pairs above.
