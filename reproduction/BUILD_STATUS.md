## What was built

- `reproduction/checks/check_volume1.py` .. `check_volume6.py` -- one independent Python
  verification script per volume, each printing concrete evidence (not just booleans) and
  ending with a `RESULT_JSON:` line listing every claim it covers, tiered per the
  workspace's own IDM discipline (`Th_coqc` / `finite_diagnostic` / `Dr` / `Open`).
- `reproduction/generate_ledger.py` -- runs all six scripts as subprocesses, parses their
  `RESULT_JSON` output, and writes `LEDGER.json` (machine-readable) + `LEDGER.md`
  (human-readable table: Volume | Claim ID | Name | Tier | Status | Evidence).
- `reproduction/reproduce_all.sh` -- one-command runner (`bash reproduce_all.sh`), exit
  code reflects whether any attempted mechanical check actually failed.
- `reproduction/README.md` -- how to reproduce, tier legend, honest scope statement, the
  Section-13 reproducibility gap, and the two document defects found (see below).
- `reproduction/LEDGER.json` / `reproduction/LEDGER.md` -- **generated by actually running
  `bash reproduce_all.sh`**, not hand-typed. Confirmed present and well-formed.
- Existing GLS-2026-006 files (`paper/`, `verification/`, `CLAIMS.md`, `README.md`,
  `LICENSE`, `.zenodo.json`, `CITATION.cff`, `registry/`, `scripts/`) were **not modified**
  except a proposed cross-reference addition to `README.md` (see below) -- confirm this is
  wanted before merging; nothing was force-changed.

## Volume-2-vs-prior-content finding (from the extraction phase, carried forward)

The Volumes 2-3 extraction agent ran `diff -u` between the extracted Volume 2 text
(pp.16-23 of the combined PDF) and `pdftotext -layout ~/Downloads/State_Breakdown_Math_Only_v0_2.pdf`
(the file GLS-2026-006's public repo was built from) and found them **identical in body
content**, differing only by a trailing-newline pdftotext artifact. Practical
consequence for this reproduction system: Volume 2's claims and GLS-2026-006's own
`State_Breakdown_Math_Only_v0_2` claims are the SAME artifact, not two -- the ledger treats
Volume 2's finite witness (Thm 3.1) as the canonical site for that claim and Volume 1's
NSR-01 / Volume 3's Thm 9.1 as restatements of it (all three ARE independently re-derived
by the check scripts, but the ledger's evidence text calls out the duplication so a reader
does not triple-count "one 8-state witness" as three separate results).

## Document defects found during extraction (flagged, not fixed)

Three internal cross-reference slips were found in the source PDFs' own proof text (neither
affects the mechanical checks above, since those check the finite mathematics directly
rather than trusting the prose's citation). The first two sit in the very same proof
sentence in Volume 2 Theorem 11.1 and were caught in two separate passes (see the
independent-review note below) -- both are now recorded together:

1. **Volume 2, Theorem 11.1's proof** cites "Theorem 9.1" -- no such numbered theorem
   exists in Volume 2 (Section 9 there is an unnumbered open-problem discussion). Likely a
   typo for Theorem 8.1, which does prove the needed H_R implication.
2. **Volume 2, Theorem 11.1's proof** (same sentence as #1) also cites "Proposition 11.1"
   -- no such proposition exists in Volume 2 (only Proposition 10.1 and 10.2 exist). Likely
   a typo for Proposition 10.1 ("Bounded retained state does not decide task adequacy"),
   whose stated content is a near-verbatim match for what the proof describes. This one was
   missed in the initial extraction pass and only surfaced during independent adversarial
   review (2026-09-09) re-reading the same paragraph against the PDF.
3. **Volume 6, Theorem 6.1's proof** cites "Theorem 2.1" -- no such numbered theorem exists
   in the extracted pages (Section 2 only contains Lemma 2.1). The proof also appears to
   conflate Theorem 4.1 (zero-defect identities) with Theorem 5.1 (horizon sufficiency)
   when justifying "exact finite-domain closure and zero defects." Re-checked line-by-line
   against the PDF during the same 2026-09-09 review pass; no further defects found in this
   proof paragraph.

All three are recorded on the affected claim's `evidence` field in `LEDGER.json`/`LEDGER.md`
and flagged here for the founder/author to correct in a future revision of the source PDF.
This reproduction system does not and should not silently patch a citation in someone
else's paper.

## Known reproducibility gap (not a defect in this system -- a genuine gap in the source)

Volume 2 Section 13's worked numerical example specifies tau_R=0.7 and states
lambda_max(L_R)=4 for an 8-node cycle graph (both independently confirmed exactly here),
and reports a 4-row error table with successive ratios ~2.0 (1.99976, 2.00069, 2.00053)
confirming `O(h)` convergence for `T=1`. **The prose does not specify the forcing vector
G_R or initial condition I_R(0)** used to generate that specific table. A naive
constant/kernel-aligned forcing trivializes the test (near-machine-precision "error" in
every mode, not the reported O(h) behavior). `checks/check_volume2.py` documents this gap
explicitly and instead verifies the underlying textbook claim (explicit Euler is globally
`O(h)` for this well-posed linear ODE) with an explicit, stated, non-kernel-aligned `G_R`
and `I_0` -- confirmed ratios land near 2.0 (1.86, 1.93, 1.97, approaching 2.0 as expected
for a coarse `h`), but this does **not** reproduce the paper's own exact published numbers.
**Recommendation for the next pass or the founder/author**: supply the exact `G_R`, `I_0`
used in the original Section 13 computation so the published table itself can be bit-for-bit
reproduced, or accept this documented substitute as sufficient evidence for the general
`O(h)` claim.
